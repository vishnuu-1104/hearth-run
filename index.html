Hearth Run

A webcam-controlled dash through an endless pastel house.

Run corridor to living room to bedroom and back again, dodging ottomans, cabinets, and cushions while collecting warm little glow-lights. The twist: your own body sets the pace — march and swing your arms in front of the camera to speed up.

How you control it

  • March / swing your arms — build speed (keep it rhythmic!)
  • Lean left / right — change lanes
  • Raise both hands up together — jump over cushions
  • Squat down — slide under tables

No camera, or prefer not to use one? The full game also works with just a keyboard.

Choose how to play

Full-body mode

Stand where your hands AND legs are visible. Marching in place plus arm swings gives the strongest speed boost.

Seated mode

Sitting at your desk? Arm movements alone control your speed, with the same lean-to-steer and raise-to-jump controls.

Keyboard mode

No camera required. Hold a key to build speed, arrows to steer and jump.

Before we turn on your camera

Hearth Run tracks your body position to control the game — this needs camera access. Your video is processed entirely on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent anywhere, and you can turn the camera off at any time from the pause menu.

Quick practice

Try each control once — it checks off automatically.

  • ✓ March / swing your arms until the energy meter fills past halfway Hold Shift (or E) until the energy meter fills past halfway
  • ✓ Lean left Press ← or A — move to the left lane
  • ✓ Lean right Press → or D — move to the right lane
  • ✓ Raise both hands above your shoulders and hold briefly Press ↑, W, or Space — jump
  • ✓ Squat down (or lower your upper body, if seated) and hold briefly Press ↓ or S — slide under tables

Press Esc or P any time to pause.
